## Mathematics practice

For new Mathematics sets, keep the supplied English questions, answer order, numbering, and diagrams. Recreate plots and diagrams as lightweight SVG and tables as HTML. Use structured MathML for vertical fractions, radicals, powers, and matrices. Break long calculations into readable steps and check them on a narrow screen.

Write for a beginner: define the relevant rule, show where the numbers or relationships come from, explain each algebraic step, and check the answer. Use an annotated figure when it helps.
